# One Very Important Question

A goofy little page for asking someone out.

- The **No** button runs away every time she tries to click it, with a new joke each time.
- The **Yes** button keeps getting bigger.
- Saying yes opens a planner to pick the **day, time and vibe**.
- After locking in the date she types her name, which is saved so Eddie can see who said yes.
- Signing the ticket creates a **PDF date ticket** and opens the share sheet so she can send it to Eddie on WhatsApp. On a computer, the PDF is saved and WhatsApp opens on Eddie's chat with the confirmation message ready.

The WhatsApp number lives in `EDDIE_WHATSAPP` near the top of the script in `index.html` (international format).

## Who said yes

`names.html` lists everyone who signed a ticket. It asks for a passcode (kept out of this repo).
